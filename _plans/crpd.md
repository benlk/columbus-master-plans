The City's Recreation and Parks Department has a [section of their website dedicated to long-range plans](https://columbusrecparks.com/connect/long-range-plans/). Currently listed are:

- [The 2023-2028 CRPD Land Plan](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/399eb0941bec432883167588c95afe73), covering land acquisition and future development. This plan builds on the 2014-2024 Land Plan, and plans for 2,874 acres of additional parkland and 180 miles of trails.
- [The Aquatics Capital Improvement Plan](https://columbusrecparks.com/connect/about/capital-improvement-projects/aquatics-capital-improvement-plan/), covering the next 20 years of
- [Playbook Columbus](https://www.playbookcolumbus.com/), a 10-year "vision plan" to guide future planning efforts.
