The Columbus Growth Strategy provides a city-wide land use map and land use categories, as well as a vision for future development within the City. It supersedes the C2P2 planning framework partially adopted in 2018, and the individual neighborhood-by-neighborhood categories and land-use maps that covered parts of the rest of the City of Columbus. It also provides city-wide design guidelines for residential, mixed-use, commercial, business/institutional, and manufacturing/industrial properties.

The Columbus Growth Strategy was adopted during "Phase Two" of the Zone In effort to update Columbus' zoning codes, to provide updated land-use recommendations that could underlay future phases of the zoning code update. When initially adopted, it focused on mixed-use, business, institutional, manufacturing, industrial, and warehouse uses.
